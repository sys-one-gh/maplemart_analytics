PRIMARY TASK E - 5 MANDATORY VIEWS (Day 3)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Create Database/Views/07_CreateViews.sql with these 5 views:

1. vwCustomerAnalytics  *** MOST IMPORTANT - THIS IS THE ML INPUT ***
   One row PER CUSTOMER (not per transaction). Must include at
   minimum:
   - CustomerID, Age (use Brian's ufnCustomerAge or the Age column),
     Province, LoyaltyLevel/MembershipLevel
   - Number of Transactions (COUNT from SalesTransaction)
   - Total Amount Spent (SUM of TransactionTotal)
   - Average Purchase Value (AVG of TransactionTotal)
   - Days Since Last Purchase (use Brian's
     ufnDaysSinceLastPurchase or DATEDIFF from MAX(TransactionDate))
   - Number of Campaigns Received (COUNT from CampaignResponse)
   - Campaign Response Rate (use Brian's ufnCampaignResponseRate,
     or SUM(PurchaseCompleted)/COUNT(*) from CampaignResponse)
   - Number of Distinct Products Purchased
   - Average Discount Received (AVG(Discount) from
     SalesTransactionItem)
   - PurchaseCompleted (the target variable Sahil's model will
     predict - pull the most recent CampaignResponse.PurchaseCompleted
     per customer, or however your team decides to define "did this
     customer respond positively," document your choice)
   Build this with LEFT JOINs so customers with zero transactions
   or zero campaign responses still appear (with 0/NULL handled
   sensibly, e.g. ISNULL(..., 0)).

2. vwCampaignPerformance
   One row per campaign: CampaignID, CampaignName, Channel,
   customers contacted, response rate, purchase completion rate,
   total revenue generated, average purchase amount.

3. vwSalesPerformance
   Sales summarized by month, by store, and by product category
   (three grouping dimensions - either one view with all three
   columns and GROUP BY ROLLUP, or clearly document your grouping
   choice).

4. vwCustomerLifetimeValue
   CustomerID, total historical spend, number of transactions,
   customer tenure (days since RegistrationDate), and a simple CLV
   calculation (e.g. total spend, or total spend / tenure * some
   projection - document your formula).

5. vwPredictionResults
   Joins Customer + CustomerPrediction + (their most recent)
   CampaignResponse so Power BI can compare actual vs predicted
   outcomes side by side.

TEST each view individually with SELECT TOP 100 * FROM
<viewname> after creating it, and sanity-check the numbers look
reasonable (no negative spend, no response rates above 100%, etc.)
